## 3. Non-negotiables

1. <b>Lock the date, time and time zone on the call.</b> It goes in the recap and the Notion calendar the same day, and everything is planned backward from it.
2. <b>Every task is a Notion card with one owner and a due date.</b> Slack is for exceptions.
3. <b>Daily client update in launch weeks</b>, even when nothing is finished.
4. <b>A deadline is slipping → DM Allan, starting with "URGENT ATTENTION".</b>
5. <b>QA before review:</b> visuals go through Maya before Allan, and nothing reaches the client untested.
6. <b>Design freeze at T-3 days.</b> Late requests become post-launch fixes.
7. <b>Nothing goes live early.</b> Build in an unpublished theme. Every go-live switch has one named owner and a time.
8. <b>Drop emails: Smart Sending OFF</b>, fixed send time.
9. <b>No dev work without a timesheet line, an estimate and Allan's approval.</b>
10. <b>Content: we brief, direct and give notes.</b> The client's editor edits.

- [ ] Products visible with the right prices; no €0
- [ ] New Arrivals = new drop only; Best Sellers + homepage match the approved layout
- [ ] PDPs: images, stock status, shipping timeline, holiday labels
