## 3. Golden rules (learned the hard way on Drop 2)

1. <b>Lock date, time and time zone on the client call and write it in the recap.</b> Then build the full calendar backward from it in Notion that same day: paid, email, web and merch in one sequence. Every time the date moves, everything downstream slips.
2. <b>Every task lives in Notion with an owner and a due date.</b> Slack is for exceptions. If it isn't on the board, it isn't being tracked.
3. <b>Flag slipping deadlines to Allan immediately by DM, starting with "URGENT ATTENTION".</b> Don't wait for him to find it in the channel.
4. <b>Design freeze at T-3 days.</b> No new design revisions in launch week unless they fix something broken. If the client asks, the launch lead pushes back and offers a post-launch fix.
5. <b>Nothing goes live early.</b> Build all homepage and theme changes in an unpublished theme or preview. Ask "who publishes this, and when?" for every change. On Drop 2 the homepage changes went live hours before launch by accident.
6. <b>Internal QA before review.</b> Nothing visual goes to Allan until Maya has checked it, and nothing goes to the client until KR8 has tested it.
7. <b>Smart Sending is always OFF for drop emails.</b> They are timed to the drop. Use a standard scheduled send at a fixed time, not Gradual/time-zone send.
8. <b>Name one person for every launch-day switch</b> (password on, products live, emails sent, password off), with the exact time and time zone.
9. <b>All dev work goes in the dev timesheet with an estimate, approved by Allan, before work starts.</b> Critical launch fixes get approved immediately, but they still go on the sheet.
10. <b>On content our job is to brief, direct and give feedback, not to edit.</b> Point the client's editor to the right footage with notes. Don't pull clips ourselves.

## 5. Email sequence

| Email | When | Who gets it | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tease | Day before Early Access | Whole list | "Something is coming" + how early access works. Don't ask people to sign up for early access in an email that only goes to people already signed up |
| Early Access | T-0, at the early access time | Subscribers | Contains the password (or private link). Example subject: "Your Drop 2 early access has arrived." |
| Launch / General Access | T+1, at the launch time | Whole list | Example subject: "Drop 2 is now LIVE" |

<callout icon="📌" color="gray_bg">
The early access email is a <b>campaign</b>, not an automated flow. Anyone who subscribes after it sends won't get the password, and the signup pop-up is off while the site is locked. So push signups hard <b>before</b> T-0 (ads, social, tease email), and tell the client this ahead of time.
</callout>

## 10. Drop 2 lessons → what we do now

| What happened on Drop 2 | Rule now |
| --- | --- |
| Launch date was agreed on the call but not tracked; Allan had to drive it to the finish line | Date goes in the recap and the Notion calendar the same day, with every task carded (Rule 1–2) |
| Deadlines slipped without anyone telling Allan | DM Allan with "URGENT ATTENTION" (Rule 3) |
| Client asked for email design revisions in launch week | Design freeze at T-3 (Rule 4) |
| Homepage changes went live hours before launch | Unpublished theme only, named publisher (Rule 5) |
| Confusion over who turns on the password page and who makes products live | Named owner + time for every switch (Rule 8, Section 7) |
| Smart Sending was on for the early access email | Always off for drop emails (Rule 7) |
| Klaviyo has no "CET" time zone | Use Europe/Brussels or convert to PST, and write both in the run of show |
| Password page first had no box to enter the password | Password box + Enter Store button is the default build |
| Customers couldn't sign up for the password while the site was locked | Push signups before T-0; tell the client in advance (Section 5) |
| Products had €0 prices and identical dimensions across sizes | Client checklist + KR8 product QA in Phase 2 |
| Switzerland, Australia, NZ, Canada were missing from shipping zones | Check zones against "where we ship" in Phase 2 |
| Wave 1 videos arrived with no audio | Audio check on asset delivery (Phase 1) |
| Old products were left in New Arrivals | New Arrivals = new drop only (Phase 2 + QA) |
| KR8 was being asked to update product tags and shipping costs | Client SOPs handed over; these are client tasks |
